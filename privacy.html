2. What AI Sponsor is, and what it is not

AI Sponsor is an AI-powered support companion delivered over WhatsApp. It offers check-ins, encouragement, and conversation for people in recovery.

It is not medical care, not therapy, not counselling, and not a crisis service. It is not staffed by licensed professionals, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Alcoholics Anonymous, Narcotics Anonymous, or any twelve-step programme or organisation.

If you are in crisis, text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line) or call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). In an emergency, call 911.

3. What we collect

You give us directly

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, which is how we identify your conversation and reply to you
  • Your messages, both text and voice notes
  • Whatever you choose to share in conversation, which will often include details about your recovery, substance use, mental health, and personal life
  • Your preferences, such as the name and voice you choose for your sponsor
  • Registration details if you sign up through the website

Generated as you use the service

  • A conversation summary. To keep continuity without holding every message forever, older parts of your conversation are periodically condensed into a rolling summary of durable facts: your name, the people who matter to you, your triggers, your milestones. This summary is what allows your sponsor to still recognise you if you come back after a long gap.
  • Message metadata, meaning timestamps and delivery status
  • Activity records, meaning which days you engaged, used for your streak and progress
  • Billing records, held by Stripe

We do not collect

Your legal name, home address, date of birth, or government ID. We never see or store your full card details, which go directly to Stripe.

4. How AI processes your conversations

This is the section we most want you to read.

When you message AI Sponsor, the content is sent to third-party AI providers so a reply can be generated:

  • Anthropic (Claude) generates the conversational responses
  • OpenAI transcribes the voice notes you send, so they can be processed as text
  • xAI turns replies into spoken voice notes
  • Earlier parts of your conversation, and the rolling summary described above, are sent as context with each request, so replies make sense and remember what you have told us

AI-generated responses can be wrong, incomplete, or badly suited to your situation. AI Sponsor cannot assess risk, diagnose anything, or intervene in an emergency.

7. How long we keep your information

Your conversation history is kept while your account is active.

If an account goes twelve months with no activity, the raw conversation messages are deleted. The rolling summary described in Section 3 is kept beyond that point.

We want to be plain about why it works this way. People in recovery go quiet sometimes and come back, and losing someone's whole history over a normal gap would be the wrong outcome. But holding onto verbatim disclosures indefinitely is not right either. Deleting the literal conversation while keeping a short factual summary is the line we have drawn: your sponsor can still recognise you, without us keeping your exact words forever.

Billing records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

What we cannot promise: our database and hosting providers, Neon and Render, keep their own infrastructure backups for a period, as is standard practice. That is outside our direct control, and we would rather say so than imply that no trace exists anywhere.

8. Your choices and rights

At any time you can:

  • Stop using the service, simply by not messaging
  • Clear your history using “start over”
  • Request a copy of what we hold about you
  • Request deletion of everything
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

To do any of these, ask your sponsor for your settings link, or use the Delete my data form on our website. We respond within 30 days.

What deletion actually does. When you ask, we give you a short window to change your mind, and tell you the date it will happen. Nothing is removed during that window and your sponsor keeps working, so a request made in a bad moment is not irreversible. After it, we cancel your subscription with Stripe first, then purge your messages, summary, profile and activity records from our database, then remove your contact from GoHighLevel. We send you one message confirming it is done. It is complete, including the summary that outlives the twelve-month window.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights under the GDPR (UK and EEA) or CCPA/CPRA (California), including the right to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

We do not use this information for anything other than operating the service for you.

10. Security

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you and any relevant regulator as the law requires.
