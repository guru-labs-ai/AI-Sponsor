Something to listen to, somewhere to go: Debtors Anonymous at 50

By the AI Sponsor team • Published 17 October 2026 • 4 min read

This year Debtors Anonymous turned 50. To mark it, DA held its first worldwide virtual convention on 30 and 31 May 2026. DA says more than 2,000 people from 43 countries joined, with over 100 speakers in seven languages. Most of it was recorded, and DA has now put the recordings online for anyone to hear, free.

There are 33 sessions. Nobody needs all of them. These are the five we would start with, depending on where you are right now. Further down are this autumn's DA gatherings, one of them still to come.

Five talks to start with

  • If you are new

    Finding Your Way: Navigating the First 30 Days

    A panel of four members on the first month in DA, put together by the Greater New York Intergroup. Good company if you have been to one meeting and are not sure about the second.

    ▶ Listen
  • If you run a business

    The Soulful CEO: Running a Spiritually Sustainable Business

    The Business Debtors Anonymous session, organized by DA's world service BDA committee with members from Mexico and the US. For anyone whose debt and business are tangled together.

    ▶ Listen
  • If earning is the sore spot

    Income with Integrity: Living our Best Lives with Spiritually Sustainable Earning

    Members from South Africa, the US and Finland on earning in a way that does not cost you your recovery.

    ▶ Listen
  • With the holidays coming

    Joy Without Debt: Planning for Holidays, Travel, and Beyond

    For many people the holidays are when spending gets away from them. Worth hearing in October, before the gift lists start, not in January.

    ▶ Listen
  • If your health is part of the story

    Chronic Illness, Constant Hope: Staying Solvent Through Health Challenges

    Hosted by members of DA's Tuesday Health Issues Meeting, for when the bills come from being unwell.

    ▶ Listen

The rest of the list is on DA's convention recordings page, including sessions in Spanish, Portuguese and Russian. DA notes that speaker shares are personal experience and are not statements of DA policy. For older recordings, DA keeps a library of speaker shares going back to 2018.

If you would rather be in a room

Recordings are good at 1am. But there is something a speaker share cannot do, which is sit next to you. One gathering still to come this autumn, and one that has just passed:

New York time · Sunday 8 November 2026

Service in Gratitude workshop, DA of Greater New York

Three speakers on DA service from 2:00 to 3:30pm Eastern, then a newcomers' session from 3:40 to 4:00pm. Suggested donation is $13, and the organizers say no one is turned away for lack of funds. You register by email. Their page does not say whether it is in person or online, so ask when you write.

Details and how to register

London · Saturday 24 October 2026 · now past

"Living without Debt", London DA Fellowship Day

A full day, 9:30am to 5pm, hosted by a London DA meeting at Bloomsbury Central Baptist Church, 235 Shaftesbury Avenue, near Covent Garden, with chairs on both personal and business debting.

DA UK's page for the day

We took these dates and details from each group's own page on 17 October 2026 and checked them again on 26 October. Plans change, so check the page before you travel.
