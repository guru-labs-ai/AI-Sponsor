Something to join, something to read: Alcoholics Anonymous

By the AI Sponsor team • Published 11 November 2026 • 4 min read

The weeks around the holidays can be some of the hardest of the year. Alcoholics Anonymous has a lot to lean on that costs nothing, and nearly all of it sits on AA's own websites. Here is what is coming up, and where to find it.

A free online convention, 20 to 22 November

Online · Friday 20 to Sunday 22 November 2026

OIAA2026 Online International Convention

The Online Intergroup of Alcoholics Anonymous (OIAA) is the AA service body set up to help online AA groups carry the message. Its 2026 international convention is online, the weekend before Thanksgiving. The theme is "Recovery, Service and Unity", and each of the three days spotlights a different region of the world.

Attendance is free. OIAA welcomes contributions to keep the event self-supporting, in line with AA's Seventh Tradition. You register with an email address and get a confirmation by email. The schedule for all three days is out, some meetings are interpreted in ASL, and there are registration flyers in Spanish, French, Chinese, Persian and Portuguese.

Read, watch or listen: AA's own books, free on aa.org

AA keeps several of its books on aa.org to read free, chapter by chapter. Some also have Watch and Listen options, including videos in American Sign Language. Which one to open depends on where you are right now.

  • If you are new

    Alcoholics Anonymous, the Big Book

    AA's basic text. It was first published in 1939 to show how the first hundred members got sober, and AA says it is now translated into more than 70 languages. The first two chapters are "Bill's Story" and "There Is a Solution".

    ▶ Read it on aa.org
  • If you are working the steps

    Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions

    Known as the "Twelve and Twelve", it has a chapter for each Step and each Tradition. Open the chapter for the step you are on.

    ▶ Read it on aa.org
  • If the holidays are hard

    Living Sober

    AA calls it a practical booklet about how members stay sober one day at a time. It has an audio version, chapter by chapter, with chapters like "Using the 24-hour plan" and "Changing old routines".

    ▶ Read or listen on aa.org
  • If you want something each morning

    Daily Reflections

    A short reading for each day of the year, on aa.org.

Meetings on your phone

AA's free Meeting Guide app, for iPhone and Android, lists more than 150,000 weekly meetings, in person and online. AA says the listings come from more than 500 local AA service offices and are refreshed twice a day, and the app shows a daily quote from Daily Reflections. Get Meeting Guide.

For online meetings only, OIAA keeps its own directory, including meetings happening right now. Find an online meeting.

We took these dates and details from AA's and OIAA's own pages on 11 November 2026. Plans change, so check their pages before you plan around them.
