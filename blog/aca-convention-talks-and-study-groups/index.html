Something to listen to, someone to work with: Adult Children of Alcoholics

By the AI Sponsor team • Published 26 October 2026 • 4 min read

Adult Children of Alcoholics and Dysfunctional Families (ACA) held its 2026 Annual World Convention online on 27 and 28 March. The theme was "Transformation from Hurting to Healing to Helping". ACA says it had 17 workshops and panels, presented by members from around the world, and the recordings are now on ACA's own store.

One thing has changed since the spring. From 1 October 2026, ACA's World Service Organization charges $3 for each downloadable recording. It charged the same before COVID, made newer recordings free during it, and says bringing the fee back helps cover the cost of keeping its audio library going. So these are $3 a talk, paid to ACA, not to us.

Nobody needs all 17. These are the five we would start with, depending on where you are right now.

Five talks to start with

  • If the Laundry List sounds like you

    3 Keys for Transforming & Healing the Laundry Lists

    ACA describes it as a workshop about healing the Laundry List traits: the fourteen traits ACA says many adult children have in common.

    ▶ Listen ($3)
  • If an old voice still criticizes you

    Identifying and Reframing Critical Childhood Messages

    In ACA's words, a workshop about identifying the critical messages heard in childhood.

    ▶ Listen ($3)
  • If you are raising children

    Parenting While ReParenting

    ACA describes it as a workshop about parenting your children while reparenting the child inside you. For anyone doing both at once.

    ▶ Listen ($3)
  • If recovery has started to feel like homework

    Permission to Play: Healing the Inner Child Through Joy

    A workshop about healing the inner child through joy and play.

    ▶ Listen ($3)
  • If your body is carrying it

    Finding Safety in the Body Through the 5 Senses

    A workshop about finding safety in the body, through the five senses.

    ▶ Listen ($3)

The rest are on ACA's 2026 convention page. ACA also keeps the recordings of its earlier world conventions, going back to 2016, on its audio recordings page.

If $3 a talk is not possible right now, the reading is free. The Laundry List is on ACA's site, and ACA has free literature to download in more than 30 languages.

If you would rather work through it with others

In most 12-step programs the deep work happens one to one, with a sponsor. ACA's site says adult children have also found another way: small study groups that meet regularly to work through an ACA workbook together, in person, online or by phone. ACA's Fellowship Text, the Big Red Book, calls this "indirect sponsorship".

Two things are worth knowing before you look. New groups are listed only while they are taking members, and most close to newcomers after the first several meetings. ACA says that is on purpose, so the group can build safety and trust. And a study group sits alongside regular ACA meetings: ACA says it does not replace them.

Find a study group on ACA's site. You can filter by in person, online or telephone, and by time zone.

And the hours in between

A recording helps when you can't sleep. A study group helps when you are ready to do the work with other people. A lot of the hard moments happen somewhere else: after a phone call with family, or when an old reaction takes over and you only notice it later.

That is where an AI sponsor fits. You can text it at any hour, say what just happened, and work out one small next step to take to your next meeting or study group. It can also look up online ACA meetings for you, from ACA's own meeting search, with the time where you are.
